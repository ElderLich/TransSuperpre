Odd-Eyes Override Dragon

2+ monsters, including a Pendulum Monster
You can target 1 other card you control; destroy it, and if you do, draw 1 card. During your turn, when your opponent activates a card or effect (Quick Effect): You can discard 1 card; negate that effect. You can only use each of the preceding effects of "Odd-Eyes Override Dragon" once per turn. If you destroyed a Pendulum Monster Card by this card's first effect, during your Main Phase this turn, you can conduct 1 Pendulum Summon of a "Performapal" monster(s), "Magician" Pendulum Monster(s), and/or "Odd-Eyes" monster(s), in addition to your Pendulum Summon (you can only gain this effect once per turn).
