[name]
Angelechy Verdict

[desc]
Apply this effect based on the number of "Angelechy" Monster Cards on the field.
● 1 to 4: Negate the effects of 1 face-up card your opponent controls.
● 5+: Banish all cards your opponent controls.
You can banish this card from your GY; send 1 "Angelechy" card from your hand or face-up field to the GY, then you can place 1 "Angelechy" monster with a different name than the cards in your Spell & Trap Zone, from your Extra Deck in your Spell & Trap Zone as a face-up Continuous Spell. You can only use each effect of "Angelechy Verdict" once per turn.
