If 4 or more cards are on the field: You can Special Summon this card from your hand. You can send up to 2 Spells/Traps you control to the GY; draw that many cards, then you can Special Summon 1 WATER monster from your hand. You can only use each effect of "Audhumla, Progenitor of the Frozen Expanse" once per turn.
