Autumn

[desc]
This card's name becomes "Summer" while in the Field Zone. Once per turn: You can place Season Counters on this card equal to the number of cards in your opponent's hand. At the start of the Battle Phase: You can take 1 Field Spell from your hand, Deck, or GY that you can place a Season Counter on, except "Autumn", and place it face-up on your field, and if you do, place all Season Counters on this card on that card. If a card(s) in the Field Zone would be destroyed by card effect, you can banish this card from your GY instead.
