Sinful Spoils of the White Crow Forest

When your opponent activates a card or effect (Quick Effect): You can return this card to the hand, then target 1 monster in the face-up field or either GY; place it face-up in its owner's Spell & Trap Zone as a Continuous Spell. You can only use this effect of "Sinful Spoils of the White Crow Forest" once per turn.
