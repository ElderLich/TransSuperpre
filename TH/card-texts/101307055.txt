Reindsurm Conquest

[desc]
This card can be used to Ritual Summon any number of Insect Ritual Monsters. You must also shuffle Insect and/or Dragon monsters from the GY(s) into the Deck whose total Levels equal or exceed the total Levels of the Ritual Monsters. If this card is in your GY: You can send 1 Insect monster from your hand or face-up field to the GY; add this card to your hand. You can only use each effect of "Reindsurm Conquest" once per turn.
