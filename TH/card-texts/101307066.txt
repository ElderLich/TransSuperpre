Bunnillin Extract

[desc]
While you control 2 or more non-Token Normal Monsters, draw 2 cards instead of 1 for your normal draw during your Draw Phase. If a Level 5 or higher Normal Monster(s) with 2000 or less original ATK/DEF is Special Summoned or declares an attack: You can target 1 card your opponent controls; destroy it.
