[name]
Cubic Bodhi

[desc]
You can activate only 1 card with this card's name per turn.
(1) When this card resolves, you can send 1 "Cubic" card from your Deck to the GY.
(2) Once per turn: You can discard 1 card; place 1 Cubic Counter on 1 face-up monster your opponent controls. (Monsters with a Cubic Counter cannot attack, also negate their effects.)
(3) If a "Cubic" monster(s) is Special Summoned to your field: You can Fusion Summon 1 "Cubic" monster from your Extra Deck, using monsters from your hand or field.
